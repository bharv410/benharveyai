Common questions

  • Is linking my bank safe? Yes. Bank linking is handled by Plaid and is read-only — BHarv Finance AI can never move your money, and we never see your bank password. You can disconnect at any time from your bank, or delete your data (below).
  • How do I delete my account and data? In the app: open the menu → Settings → Delete account. It removes your profile, chats, transactions, and bank links. Works for guest accounts too — no email required.
  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a reset link. Guest users don't need a password at all.
  • Bank disconnected or numbers look stale? Open the menu and tap "Connect your bank" to re-link — the app will also prompt you if it detects a broken connection.
  • Does BHarv Finance AI sell my data or show ads? No. No ads, no data sales, no tracking. Details in the Privacy Policy.
  • Is the coach's advice financial advice? It's educational coaching, not licensed financial, investment, or tax advice. See the in-app Disclosures (Settings → Disclosures).
