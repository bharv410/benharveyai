The Station Principle

Same question.
Four completely different answers.

"How do I make more money?" has no single right answer — it depends entirely on where you stand. Money IQ diagnoses your station from your job, income, and skills before it ever prescribes a move.

Hourly / entry

More hours, one skill.

The fastest lever is better shifts, a raise ask, and starting ONE wage-floor skill — while plugging the biggest leak so the extra earnings stick.

Salaried / mid-career

The raise or the jump.

Rarely cutting lattes. Negotiation or a 15–30% job jump beats frugality — then monetize what you already know on the side.

High earner

Leverage and ownership.

Turn expertise into equity or a productized income. Optimize taxes. Put idle cash to work instead of letting it sit in checking.

Owner / founder / CEO

Enterprise value, not salary.

Capital, margin, and equity moves that multiply the whole asset — personal spending is a rounding error.
